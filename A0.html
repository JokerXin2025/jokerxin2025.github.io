证明

等价代换 计算 $\displaystyle\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{e^{x^{2}}-\cos x}{x\sin x}$ $=$ $\displaystyle\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{e^{x^{2}}-\cos x}{x^{2}}$
洛必达法则 $\textcolor{lightgrey}=$ $\displaystyle\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{e^{x^{2}}\cdot (2x)-(-\sin x)}{2x}$
化简 $=$ $\displaystyle\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{e^{x^{2}}\cdot (2x)+\sin x}{2x}$
洛必达法则 $\textcolor{lightgrey}=$ $\displaystyle\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{e^{x^{2}}\cdot (4x^{2})+2e^{x^{2}}+\cos x}{2}$
代入 $=$ $\dfrac{3}{2}$
